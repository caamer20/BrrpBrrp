# Code of conduct

BrrpBrrp adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct. The Covenant is short and it is not reproduced here:
read it at that link, in full, before taking part. This page says only the things the
Covenant leaves to a project to decide — its scope, its contact and how it is enforced.

## Scope

The Covenant applies to every space this project runs: issues, pull requests, review
comments, commit messages and any other discussion attached to the repository or to a
release. It applies to everyone in those spaces — contributors, reviewers, maintainers and
the owner — and to anyone representing the project in public.

The project is a self-hosted server rather than a hosted service, so the Covenant does not
reach what a community does inside its own installation. Whoever operates an installation
answers for it; [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [docs/security.md](docs/security.md) describe
the boundary between the application and its operator.

## Reporting and enforcement

Send conduct or privacy reports privately to [cameron.amer@gmail.com](mailto:cameron.amer@gmail.com).
Include the incident, relevant links or dates, and the safest way to contact you. Share only
what is needed; keep personal data and accusations out of public issues. Reports are handled
by the project maintainer with discretion. Security vulnerabilities use the separate private
intake in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement follows the Covenant's own enforcement guidelines.
Decisions are recorded, the person reporting is told the outcome, and a maintainer involved
in an incident does not decide its outcome alone. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
describes how contributions are reviewed and [CLA.md](CLA.md) how rights are handled; none
of that is affected by a report made under this page.
