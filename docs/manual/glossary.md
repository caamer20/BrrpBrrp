# Glossary

Terms as the interface and these guides use them.

**Administrator.** An account with administrative abilities. Administrators can manage people,
channels and settings, and can delete messages with confirmation. In the app, they cannot read
direct conversations they are not part of. See [Administrators](administrators.md).

**Announcement channel.** A restricted channel where everyone who can read it may read, but only
people with channel-management abilities may post.

**Backup.** A verified archive of the server's database and, optionally, its uploaded files. Backups
are not encrypted. See [Administrators](administrators.md#backups-and-restore).

**Blocking.** Stopping a person from contacting you and you from contacting them, in both directions.
See [Direct messages and groups](direct-messages-and-groups.md#block-someone).

**Capability.** One ability a role can carry, such as posting in a channel, moderating messages or
managing invitations. Roles add capabilities; they never take them away.

**Category.** A named group of channels in the sidebar. Renaming a category does not change who can see
its channels.

**Channel.** A named place for a topic, either a **text** channel or a **voice** channel. See
[Channels](channels.md).

**Direct conversation.** A private conversation between two people, started by a request that the other
person accepts. See [Direct messages and groups](direct-messages-and-groups.md).

**Group.** A private conversation among several people. A person joins only by accepting an invitation.

**Invitation.** A single-use link, lasting one hour to seven days, that creates one account. It can carry
a role.

**Mention.** A message that names a person with `@` and their username. A mention can produce a popup and,
where enabled, a device notification.

**Mute.** Silencing a channel's popups and push notifications for you. Messages and unread counts still work.

**Owner.** The one account that holds the operator powers: archiving the server, recovering credentials and
transferring ownership. Ownership is passed on through an explicit ceremony, not by assigning a role.

**Pin.** Marking a message so it is listed under **Pinned messages** in its channel. A moderation action.

**Private channel.** A restricted channel readable only by the roles and accounts its managers list.
Community managers can still read it.

**Reaction.** A small emoji added to a message. Select your own reaction again to remove it.

**Recovered messages.** The place where unsent text lands when a send is refused permanently. The app never
sends recovered text for you.

**Recovery code.** One of ten single-use codes you save when turning on two-factor sign-in. Each works once,
in place of the authenticator code.

**Reply thread.** A discussion opened from one message, showing the original and its replies. Replies also
stay in the channel.

**Restricted channel.** A channel with access limited by its settings: private, or announcements-only, or
both. Its settings are visible in its access panel.

**Role.** A named set of capabilities assigned to accounts. **Administrator**, **Moderator** and **Member**
are built in.

**Screen share.** Sharing a window, tab or screen with a voice room, optionally with the sound your computer
plays. One share per room; two across the server.

**Slow mode.** A minimum interval between one member's messages in a channel, set by its managers.

**Timeout.** A moderator's temporary suspension of an account for a set number of minutes.

**Two-factor sign-in.** Signing in with a password and a code from an authenticator app.

**Upload limit.** The largest file the server will keep for one attachment. The default is 12 MB.

**Voice room.** The participants of a voice channel, who can hear each other while connected.
