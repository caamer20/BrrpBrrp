# Notifications and preferences

**For members.** This chapter explains what interrupts you, and where to change your profile,
appearance and sound settings. Open **Your preferences** with the gear beside your name at the
foot of the sidebar.

Preferences are in five tabs: **Profile**, **Appearance**, **Audio**, **Notifications** and
**Account**. Use the arrow keys to move between tabs.

## Profile

Your **Display name** is what people see in messages and member lists. Your **Avatar colour** is the
colour of your avatar. Choose **Save name and colour** to apply the change. Your username does not
change, and the change shows for everyone at once.

## Appearance

- **Theme.** New devices follow the system's light or dark setting. Choose an explicit theme to
  override it on this device.
- **Message spacing.** **Comfortable** or **Compact**.
- **Remember drafts and unsent messages on this device.** Off by default. When on, unsent text and
  messages that could not be delivered are kept between visits on this browser. Signing out clears
  them.
- **Save data & reduce motion.** Defers images to save data, and reduces motion in the interface.
- **Private previews.** Load image previews and external media only when you choose, rather than
  automatically.

## Audio

Microphone, speaker, push to talk, the join chime and the test controls are on this tab. They are
described in [Voice and screen sharing](voice-and-screen-sharing.md#before-your-first-call).

## Notifications

This tab governs what reaches you on **this browser and device**. The page says so: these settings
do not follow you to another device.

- **Mention notifications.** Choose whether a message that names you produces a notification
  popup and, if you allow push, a device notification.
- **Messages and requests.** Whether direct messages and requests produce a notification on this
  device.
- **Push status.** The page shows whether this device can receive push notifications, and
  **Check again** re-tests it after you change your browser's permission.

Push notifications come from the browser's own push service. Each device must be enabled
separately. The payload carries a notice that someone wrote to you or mentioned you, not the text
of the message. On iPhone and iPad, install the app to the Home Screen before you turn on
notifications.

### Channel mutes

Muting a channel silences its popups and push notifications for you, and nothing else. See
[Channels](channels.md#mute-a-channel).

### What is not a notification

Unread counts, mention history and the message list are always up to date. A notification that
has already reached a device cannot be recalled by changing these settings.

## Account

**Change password** is here, with the current password. Changing it signs out all your sessions, so
you sign in again on each device.

**Two-factor authentication** and **Signed-in devices** are explained in
[Your account and security](account-and-security.md).

**App updates** shows whether the server has a newer version of the app waiting. When it does, the
update notice asks you to refresh, and it waits until you are not in a call or in the middle of
sending something.
