# Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to BrrpBrrp. This agreement lets the project accept your
contribution while keeping its licensing consistent for everyone who uses it.

**Status:** This is the agreement the project asks contributors to accept. It is written
in plain language for that purpose and is not legal advice; the owner should have it
reviewed against the chosen jurisdiction before the public repository opens, and should
decide whether signatures are collected by hand or through a signing service (see
[How to sign](#how-to-sign)).

- **"You"** means the person or organization submitting a Contribution.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship you intentionally submit to the

  form and through any channel the project uses.
- **"Owner"** means the copyright holder of the project, named in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **"Project"** means BrrpBrrp and the software distributed from its canonical
  repository.

## 7. How to sign

The project uses the manual signing process below until a signing service is configured:

1. Open a pull request. In its description, include this exact sentence:

   > I have read the CLA in CLA.md and I hereby sign the CLA, and I confirm the
   > representations in it apply to every contribution in this pull request.

2. A maintainer records the signature by adding your account to the signatory list in the
   same pull request, and the pull request is merged with that file change included.
3. Later contributions from the same account are covered by that recorded signature
   unless you withdraw it in writing.

If you contribute on behalf of an organization, or through an account that represents
more than one person, describe that in the pull request and the Owner will ask for a
separate signed copy before merging.

## 8. Withdrawing

You may stop contributing at any time. The licenses in sections 3 and 4 are irrevocable
for Contributions you have already submitted, because they have already been distributed
to everyone who received the Project.
